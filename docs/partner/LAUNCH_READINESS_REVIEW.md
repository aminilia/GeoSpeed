# Launch Readiness Review

## Product Criteria

Target partner use cases are validated with scenario replay and issue triage closed or accepted.

## Data-Quality Criteria

Route coverage, speed-limit quality, freshness, and conflict scores meet agreed thresholds.

## SDK / Integration Criteria

Open simulator contracts are stable, documented, and versioned.

## ADAS Validation

Speed-limit assist behavior is suppressed for low confidence and warns on cruise-control mismatch.

## Infotainment Validation

Speed-limit display, stale-data warnings, and alert rendering fit the target screen layout.

## Partner Signoff

Launch-ready status requires no open blockers and documented partner acceptance.
