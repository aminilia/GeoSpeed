# FDE Operating Model

## Role of Forward Deployed Engineering

FDE bridges partner requirements, field debugging, product feedback, and core engineering execution.

## Partner Onboarding

Start with use-case discovery, platform constraints, route coverage goals, validation environments, and launch timeline.

## Technical Discovery

Collect route samples, signal expectations, UI constraints, ADAS behavior, and data-quality thresholds.

## Issue Triage

Classify each issue by type, severity, reproducibility, affected component, root cause, and launch impact.

## Weekly Partner Review

Review open blockers, demos, data-quality deltas, feature requests, and next launch gate.

## Escalation Path

Escalate launch blockers with reproducible scenario ID, logs, expected behavior, actual behavior, and owner recommendation.

## Handoff

Convert repeated partner needs into product requirements with acceptance criteria and evidence.

## Global Coordination

Maintain decision logs, async updates, and timezone-aware response ownership.
