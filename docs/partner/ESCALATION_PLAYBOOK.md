# Escalation Playbook

## When To Escalate

Escalate launch blockers, safety-sensitive ADAS mismatches, repeated data-quality regressions, and partner executive commitments.

## Evidence To Collect

Scenario JSON, route replay, partner issue ID, affected component, owner team, screenshot, logs, and latest release-readiness state.

## Internal Summary

State impact, partner, timeline, reproduction, suspected root cause, proposed owner, and decision needed.
