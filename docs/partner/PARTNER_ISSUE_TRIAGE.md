# Partner Issue Triage

## Issue Types

- `MAP_DATA_GAP`
- `SPEED_LIMIT_MISMATCH`
- `SDK_INTEGRATION_BUG`
- `ADAS_SIGNAL_MISMATCH`
- `INFOTAINMENT_RENDERING_ISSUE`
- `ROUTE_GUIDANCE_ISSUE`
- `LATENCY_OR_PERFORMANCE`
- `PARTNER_FEATURE_REQUEST`

## Severity Levels

- Critical: launch blocker or safety-sensitive regression.
- High: blocks validation or partner signoff.
- Medium: material issue with workaround.
- Low: minor polish or documentation gap.

## Response Targets

- Critical: same business day.
- High: one business day.
- Medium: three business days.
- Low: next planning review.

## Root-Cause Categories

Map data, inference, vehicle signal mapping, UI integration, partner configuration, performance, documentation.

## Reproducibility Checklist

Scenario ID, partner ID, route ID, signal replay, affected segment, expected behavior, actual behavior, logs, screenshot.

## Launch-Blocker Policy

Critical and unresolved high-severity ADAS or speed-limit display issues block launch readiness.
